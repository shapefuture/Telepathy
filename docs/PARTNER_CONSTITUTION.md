# Partner constitution (draft for signature between operators)

This is a starting text for two or more community operators. It isn't legal advice; have it
reviewed for your jurisdictions before real member data is exchanged.

1. **Purpose.** Partners exchange bounded requests for help, so members can get help their own
   community can't give. Nothing else: no marketing, recruiting or list-building.
2. **What travels.** Only the request type, the member-approved summary and criteria. A member's
   contact travels only after that member chose one partner's offer, and only to that partner.
3. **No forwarding.** A request may not be passed to a third community or person outside the
   helper you nominate.
4. **Member ownership.** The member stays the home community's member. Providers don't solicit
   them for membership or paid services beyond the agreed offer.
5. **Capacity is real.** Only offer slots a real, willing person has agreed to. Decline what you
   can't serve; silence is not allowed: decline or let the request expire.
6. **Withdrawal and deletion.** On a withdraw message, delete the summary and contact from your
   systems and notes, and tell your helper to do the same.
7. **Categories and limits.** Each side lists the categories it accepts. Default limit: 50
   inquiries per partner per day.
8. **Incidents.** Report any suspected disclosure or misuse to the other operator within 72 hours.
   A partner can be suspended at once (`/partner suspend`); members' rights continue.
9. **Commercial terms.** Volunteer by default. Paid, reciprocal or member-benefit terms are
   stated on each offer. No fees tied to capital raised or securities transactions.
10. **Exit.** Either side can leave at any time by suspending the other; open commitments are
    finished or released and members are told.
